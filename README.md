# PhotoProvenance
Interrogate a found copy of your image against the original and generate a report to substantiate your claim.

Some limits:
It strongly supports a claim of authorship but isn't proof in itself. Your RAW file and its camera metadata remain your best evidence.
Heavy AI re-rendering or perspective warps can defeat the matching.
Browsers can't open RAW or HEIC files, so photographers need to export a full-size JPEG of the original first.
